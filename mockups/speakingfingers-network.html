How we help

Programmes are built with Deaf youth, not only for them.

🤟

Sign language training

Practical sign language and communication skills for Deaf youth, families, schools and service providers.

🌱

Youth capacity building

Confidence, assertive communication and life skills that help young Deaf people navigate services and stand up for themselves.

📣

Community advocacy

Public education and partnerships that push for sign language access as a rights issue, not a favour.

Founded on a single encounter

SpeakingFingers Network began in June 2017 after a conversation revealed how much Deaf young people were losing because communication support was missing. It now runs programmes across three states, working with Deaf adolescents, their families, schools and communities.
